DEPENDENCY POLICY
-----------------

cargo-deny 0.20.2 evaluates the locked Rust workspace with all features and all
target-specific, build, and development edges. Unknown registries, unknown
licenses, restricted/source-available licenses, and unapproved copyleft terms
fail closed. about.toml records the selected branch for OR expressions; every
term of an AND expression is retained. Package-scoped exceptions, if present,
are visible in both about.toml and deny.toml.

The JavaScript, Python, and Go lists below record distribution roles and exact
locked direct dependencies. Changes to any list fail the repository check until
reviewed. Dependencies of separately delivered SDKs are not represented as
statically embedded in the vmon host binaries.
